01About

I'm building Corrobo, AI that banks can trust with their compliance work. Our models run on the bank's own hardware, so customer data never leaves the building, and every model is measured on documents it has never seen before it ships. An analyst signs every case.

Before Corrobo I spent 2.5 years as a data scientist at a leading Singapore bank, working on name screening, transaction monitoring and document AI, after a Master's in AI at NUS.
